Intelligence Capabilities

Six layers of field intelligence, working together

Each capability strengthens the others — satellite signals get confirmed by hyperspectral data, sensor readings, and weather context before anything reaches your dashboard.

📡

Multispectral Monitoring

Sentinel-2 style Red / NIR / Red-Edge / SWIR bands drive frequent, field-wide vegetation index tracking across your whole farm.

🔬

Hyperspectral Verification

Narrow-band hyperspectral signatures cross-check zones flagged as stressed, confirming subtle stress before it's visible to the eye.

🧠

AI Stress Prediction

A trained risk model estimates stress probability from satellite, weather and history — shown as a probability, never a diagnosis.

🌦️

Weather Monitoring

Live conditions and short-term trends are folded into the risk picture, since heat and rainfall drive much of early crop stress.

📶

IoT Sensor Intelligence

Soil moisture, temperature, pH, EC and leaf wetness readings — real or simulated — add ground-level evidence to satellite findings.

🗺️

Zone-Wise Intervention

Fields are split into zones, color-coded by health, so treatment can be targeted precisely rather than applied uniformly.
