How AgroSentinel sees your field

A layered spectral analysis pipeline turns raw satellite passes into actionable, zone-level crop health intelligence.

📡

Multispectral Monitoring

Sentinel-2 style Red / NIR / Red-Edge / SWIR bands drive frequent, field-wide vegetation index tracking (NDVI, NDRE, SAVI, NDMI).

🔬

Hyperspectral Verification

Narrow-band hyperspectral signatures cross-check zones flagged as stressed, confirming subtle stress before it's visible to the eye.

🗺️

Zone-Wise Intervention

Fields are automatically divided into zones, color-coded by health, so treatment can be targeted rather than applied uniformly.
